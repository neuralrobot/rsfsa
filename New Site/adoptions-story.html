The Adoption Story

How the Road Safety Foundation embraced CMS and VRT as family, creating a powerful partnership for road safety on South Africa's busiest route.

A Decision That Changed Everything

In 2022, the Road Safety Foundation Directors made a landmark decision that would strengthen road safety on South Africa's most critical highway - they chose to adopt both Community Medical Services (CMS) and Volunteer Rescue Team (VRT).

The Foundation of Partnership

This wasn't just a business decision - it was a recognition of shared values, common goals, and the understanding that saving lives on South African roads requires dedicated collaboration between organizations that truly care.

The Heroes We Adopted

Two extraordinary teams with decades of dedication to saving lives

Community Medical Services

42
Years of Service

Four decades of unwavering commitment to emergency medical care on the N3 route during peak traffic periods.

Van Reenens Pass Base

Strategically positioned at one of South Africa's most challenging and historically high-incident areas.

All-Volunteer Team

Every member volunteers their time, providing medical services completely free of charge to all road users.

Volunteer Rescue Team

19
Years of Service

Nearly two decades of professional medical rescue operations during peak traffic periods.

Warden Base

Operating from another critical high-incident area, providing comprehensive medical rescue services.

Rescue Specialists

Highly trained medical rescue professionals offering their expertise as volunteers during the most dangerous peak traffic periods.

The Decision Behind the Adoption

Three of the Road Safety Foundation Directors had been actively involved with both CMS and VRT, witnessing firsthand the incredible work these teams were doing. They understood the financial pressures these volunteer organizations faced - covering their own fuel, accommodation, meals, and medical supplies while providing life-saving services for free.

Financial Recognition

Understanding that running these operations had become increasingly expensive, we recognized the need to support these essential services financially.

Road Safety Component

These teams directly contribute to road safety through scene safety management, early incident warnings, and high-visibility patrols during peak traffic.

Leveraging Our Partner Network

The Road Safety Foundation believed that with our extensive partner base, we could provide the funding support these two worthy operations needed to continue their life-saving work.

Beyond Emergency Response

Community impact that extends far beyond the roadside

Community Outreach

Both teams actively work with local communities, providing essential support including food, blankets, clothing, and educational resources to those in need.

Collaborative Operations

Working closely with local traffic authorities and route patrol services, our adopted teams perform essential services that help keep the N3 route safe for all travelers.

A Partnership Built on Shared Values

This adoption represents more than financial support - it's a commitment to the shared mission of making South African roads safer. By bringing CMS and VRT into the Road Safety Foundation family, we've created a powerful alliance dedicated to saving lives, supporting communities, and ensuring that help is always available when it's needed most.
