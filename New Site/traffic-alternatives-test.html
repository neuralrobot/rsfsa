N3 Route Information

The N3 is one of South Africa's busiest highways, connecting Johannesburg to Durban via Pietermaritzburg. Critical for freight and passenger transport - perfect for road safety monitoring.

Key N3 Sections:
  • Johannesburg to Heidelberg
  • Heidelberg to Harrismith
  • Van Reenen's Pass
  • Pietermaritzburg to Durban
High Risk Areas:
  • Van Reenen's Pass (weather)
  • Mooi River Toll Plaza
  • Tugela Plaza area
  • Durban approaches
